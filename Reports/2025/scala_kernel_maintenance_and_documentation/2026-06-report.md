## Summary of Work Completed

During this period, mainly on-going work for Spark 4 support, and some more CI stabilization

- Updated and refactored the build and CI of satellite project [ammonite-spark](https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark) (which brings Spark support to Almond), in order to test and support more Spark versions in the future (https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/481, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/487, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/488, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/489, https://github.com/alexarchambault/ammonite-spark/pull/491)
- Moved the [lightweight-spark-distrib](https://github.com/almond-sh/lightweight-spark-distrib) repo, used by ammonite-spark, to the [Almond organization](https://github.com/almond-sh), and started working on making it support more Spark versions (https://github.com/almond-sh/lightweight-spark-distrib/pull/1)
- More work on stabilizing Almond's CI (https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1692, https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1693, https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1700, https://github.com/almond-sh/almond/pull/1701)
